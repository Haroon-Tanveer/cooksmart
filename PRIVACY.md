# Privacy Policy — CookSmart

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

## The short version

CookSmart has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not use
analytics, advertising or crash reporting. It does not collect, share or sell
personal information.

## What stays on your phone

Your ingredient lists, favourites, saved recipes and the endpoint you have chosen
are stored only on this device, in its private app storage. Uninstalling the app
deletes all of it. Nothing is uploaded.

## Optional live recipe generation

CookSmart can write new recipes for the ingredients you have. This feature is
**off by default** and is entirely optional.

If you turn it on, the ingredients and dish name you ask for are sent to an AI
service through a small proxy server that **you run yourself** on your own
computer. Your API key is held by that proxy and is never included in this app or
stored on your phone. If you use the bundled proxy, the request goes from your
computer to the AI provider under your own key.

While live generation is on, the text you type (ingredients, and any dish idea
you tap) is sent to that proxy. If you turn live generation off, nothing leaves
your device at all. The full recipe library works entirely offline.

## Food photographs

The recipe photographs bundled with the app come from:

- **TheMealDB** — a free recipe API, used under its own terms.
- **Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons** — used under the licence of each individual
  file, with the author and licence recorded alongside the app source.

AI-written recipes get a photograph looked up from the same two sources. No
photograph is uploaded or collected from you.

## Children

CookSmart is intended for a general audience. Because the app collects no data
from anyone, it collects none from children either.

## Your rights and choices

Since no personal information is collected, there is nothing for us to disclose,
correct or delete on request. You can erase everything the app stores at any time
by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear on the Privacy screen
inside the app and in the release notes for the version you have installed.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address given on the app's
Google Play Store listing.
