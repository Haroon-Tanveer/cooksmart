# CookSmart — Play Store listing copy

Paste-ready text for the Play Console listing. Character limits are Google's.

## App name (30 max)

```
CookSmart: Recipes & Calories
```
29 characters, one under the limit. The longer `CookSmart: 560 Recipes & Calorie
Kitchen` is 40 characters and would be rejected.

## Short description (80 max)

```
Cook what you have. 560 recipes, real photos, calories for every item.
```
70 characters. The longer version that mentions "calories for every ingredient"
is 84 and would be rejected.

## Full description (4000 max)

```
CookSmart turns the ingredients you already have into something worth eating.

Open it, tell it what is in your kitchen, and it will build a recipe around it,
then show you exactly what to buy for the few things you are missing.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• 560 recipes, each hand-checked, across Arabian, Turkish, Pakistani, fast food
  and everyday cooking. Every one has real quantities, real steps and real heat
  and timing, not just a title and a photo.

• Calorie figures on every ingredient, not just the finished dish, so you can see
  where the energy actually goes before you start cooking.

• A real photograph of every dish, bundled with the app. No waiting on a
  connection to find out what a recipe looks like.

• Cook from your phone in the kitchen. Tick ingredients off as you go and each
  step is numbered in the order you need it.

• Filter by what you want: breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert, under 20 minutes,
  or by cuisine.

• Save the recipes you like. They stay on your phone.

WORKS OFFLINE

The whole library is on your device. The app opens with no account, no sign-in
and nothing to connect to, so it works on a plane, in a market with no signal, or
anywhere else you need a recipe.

OPTIONAL LIVE RECIPES

If you run your own proxy and an AI service, CookSmart can also write a new
recipe for whatever you have, suggest dishes you have not thought of, and find a
matching photograph. That is entirely optional and off by default. Your API key
stays on your own machine, in your own proxy, and is never part of this app.

PRIVACY

No account, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics. Your ingredients,
favourites and settings never leave your device unless you explicitly turn on
live generation. The full policy is inside the app.

Perfect for anyone who opens the fridge, finds three things and does not know
what to do with them.
```
